numactl: parts of clifr's numastat

internal/query/numastat.go prints numastat's tables as numactl 2.0.19's
numastat.c does (https://github.com/numactl/numactl, tag v2.0.19): the table
layout, its column widths and the compatibility output, written anew in Go.
