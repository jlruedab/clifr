iotop: parts of clifr's iotop and of clifrd's I/O priorities

clifr's iotop prints iotop's batch output (iotop -b) as iotop 0.6 does: the
Python iotop (http://guichaz.free.fr/iotop/, https://repo.or.cz/iotop.git),
as Ubuntu (0.6-24, 0.6-42) and RHEL (0.6-18, 0.6-30) ship it. Parts of
clifr's Go code follow iotop's Python code, written anew in Go:

- internal/query/iotop.go: the sizes and rates (human_size, format_size,
  format_bandwidth, format_stats), a line's and the header's layout
  (IOTopUI.get_data, refresh_display), the default sort and its switch
  without delay accounting (sorting_keys, adjust_sorting_key), the command
  (ProcessInfo.get_cmdline) and the per-process sums (update_stats); in
  iotop/ui.py and iotop/data.py.
- internal/recorder/ioprio.go: a task's I/O priority derived from its
  scheduling policy and nice value, and its names (get_ioprio_from_sched,
  get, ProcessInfo.get_ioprio's "?dif"; iotop/ioprio.py and iotop/data.py).
