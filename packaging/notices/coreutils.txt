GNU coreutils: parts of clifr's who and df

- internal/query/who.go: who's settings and output (print_line,
  idle_string), from GNU coreutils 8.32's src/who.c
  (https://github.com/coreutils/coreutils, tag v8.32), written anew in Go.
- internal/query/df.go: df's minimum column widths (field_data), from
  src/df.c.
