util-linux: parts of clifr's last, dmesg, swapon, lsblk, lslocks, lsipc, lscpu, lsmem and lsns, and of clifrd's lock, CPU and memory block reading

clifr rebuilds the output of util-linux's tools. Some of its Go code follows
util-linux's C code closely (https://github.com/util-linux/util-linux, tags
v2.37.4 and v2.41); that code was written anew in Go:

- internal/query/last.go: last's pass over the login records
  (process_wtmp_file, list) and its output (fputc_careful), from
  login-utils/last.c (util-linux 2.32 to 2.37).
- internal/query/dmesg.go: dmesg's escaping of a record (safe_fwrite), from
  sys-utils/dmesg.c.
- internal/query/swaps.go, lsblk.go, lslocks.go, escape.go: the table layout
  of libsmartcols (column widths with and without width hints, and its
  mbs_safe_encode escaping, lib/mbsalign.c) and size_to_human_string
  (lib/strutils.c), as lsblk, lslocks and swapon print into a pipe.
- internal/query/lsipc.go: lsipc's columns, default column sets, global
  summary, -o, --id's pretty output and its time formats (make_time,
  global_set_data, do_msg, do_shm, do_sem and their _global summaries,
  print_pretty), from sys-utils/lsipc.c and ipcutils.c (2.32, 2.37, 2.39,
  2.41), and strtime_short from lib/timeutils.c (2.41).
- internal/query/lscpu.go and lscpuarm.go: lscpu's cpuinfo parsing, CPU
  types, topology, caches, NUMA nodes and vulnerabilities, its summary, -e,
  -p and -C (lscpu_read_cpuinfo, deduplicate_cputypes, cputype_read_topology,
  read_caches, read_mhz, lscpu_read_vulnerabilities, print_summary,
  print_cpus_readable, print_cpus_parsable, print_caches_readable), from
  sys-utils/lscpu.c, lscpu-cputype.c, lscpu-topology.c (2.37, 2.39, 2.41) and
  lscpu.c (2.32); the ARM implementer and part names (lscpuarm.go, generated
  from lscpu-arm.c 2.41).
- internal/query/lsmem.go: lsmem's merging of blocks (read_info,
  is_mergeable, set_split_policy), columns and summary, from
  sys-utils/lsmem.c (2.32, 2.37, 2.39, 2.41); internal/recorder/memblocks.go
  (clifrd) reads what it reads (memory_block_read_attrs).
- internal/query/lsns.go: lsns' grouping of processes by namespace, its
  columns, the process tree and its line order (add_process_to_namespace,
  show_namespaces, add_scols_line, fill_column), from sys-utils/lsns.c (2.32,
  2.37, 2.39, 2.41).
- internal/recorder/cpus.go (clifrd): what lscpu reads under /sys, and its
  hypervisor detection as an unprivileged user runs it (read_virtualization,
  read_hypervisor_cpuid), from sys-utils/lscpu-virt.c (2.41).
- internal/recorder/locks.go (clifrd): lslocks' get_filename_sz, from
  misc-utils/lslocks.c (2.41).
