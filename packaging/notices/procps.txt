procps-ng: parts of clifr's w, slabtop and ps, and of clifrd's sysctl reading

From procps-ng (https://gitlab.com/procps-ng/procps, tags v3.3.17 and v4.0.4),
written anew in Go:

- internal/query/w.go: w's choice of a session's process and its output
  (find_best_proc, print_logintime, print_time_ival7, print_from,
  print_host), from w.c (3.3.17) and src/w.c (4.0.4).
- internal/query/slabtop.go: procps 3.3's slabtop sort and its USE figure,
  from slabtop.c and proc/slab.c (3.3.17).
- internal/query/ps.go: ps's STIME and %CPU (pr_stime, and %CPU over
  elapsed_seconds), from src/ps/output.c (4.0.4).
- internal/recorder/sysctl.go (clifrd): sysctl -a's skipped names
  (DEPRECATED) and slashdot, from src/sysctl.c (4.0.4).
