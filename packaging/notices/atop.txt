atop: parts of clifr's atop

clifr's atop prints atop's screens as atop 2.7.1 and 2.12.1 do into a pipe
(https://github.com/Atoptool/atop, tags v2.7.1 and v2.12.1). Parts of its Go
code follow atop's C code, written anew in Go:

- internal/query/atopfmt.go: the value formats (val2valstr, val2elapstr,
  val2cpustr, val2memstr, various.c), the layout of a system line
  (showsysline, showsys.c) and the parsing of line definitions (makeargv,
  showlinux.c).
