iproute2: parts of clifr's ip and nstat

From iproute2 (https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/network/iproute2/iproute2.git),
written anew in Go:

- internal/query/iplink.go: the link type names (lib/ll_types.c) and the
  statistics layout (print_stats64, ip/ipaddress.c).
- internal/query/ipaddr.go: the address lines (print_addrinfo, ip/ipaddress.c).
- internal/query/iproute.go: the route selection and flags (filter_nlmsg,
  print_rt_flags, ip/iproute.c).
- internal/query/nstat.go: nstat's leaving out of some counters
  (useless_numbers, misc/nstat.c).
